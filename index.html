Products

Blanket

iPhone app · adaptive white noise for sleep

A sound for people who sleep next to noise: a partner who snores, a street, a neighbour's television. The app does not play recordings. It makes its sound on the phone, and it shapes that sound to cover the noise around you at the lowest volume that works. For four seconds the microphone measures your surroundings and reduces what it hears to eight numbers. Nothing is recorded, and nothing leaves the phone.

The app requires iOS 17 or later. It is in preparation for the App Store.
