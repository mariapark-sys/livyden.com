The first app · iPhone · iOS 17 or later

Blanket

A sound for people who sleep beside noise. It is made on the phone, not played from a file. For four seconds the microphone measures your surroundings, and the sound is shaped to cover them at the lowest volume that works. Leave Adapt on and it keeps measuring through the night, five seconds a minute. Nothing is recorded. Nothing leaves the phone.

Sixteen scenes. Seven named for the noise: Partner, Street, Neighbours, Home, Office, Ring, Drip. Nine for a sound: Fan, Rain, Storm, Ocean, Wind, Campfire, Stream, Train, Cabin.
