The microphone

Blanket shapes its sound to cover the noise around you. To do that, it needs to know how loud that noise is in each of eight frequency bands. The microphone is the instrument that measures this. The app opens it in three situations, and in no others.

The eight numbers are sound levels: one value per octave band from 63 Hz to 8 kHz. They describe how loud the surroundings are at each pitch. They do not contain speech, and no audio can be rebuilt from them. The audio samples themselves exist only in memory during the analysis, which takes a fraction of a second, and are then overwritten.

Concretely, the app never does any of these things:

  • It never records audio to a file.
  • It never sends audio, or anything derived from audio, to a server.
  • It never keeps audio in memory longer than the analysis needs.
  • It never opens the microphone while "Adapt to surroundings" is off.

The orange microphone indicator in the status bar is controlled by iOS, not by the app. It shows the truth: on a quiet night it lights for about five seconds each minute.

You can withdraw microphone access at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone. The app then plays without measuring, and the sound keeps the shape of the scene you chose.

What is stored on the phone

The app saves a small amount of information in its own storage on your phone, so that it can start the way you left it. This information never leaves the phone and is deleted when you delete the app.

Your settings
The scene, the tone, the volume, the "Can rise" limit and the sensitivity for Adapt, and the other switches in Settings.
The last measurement
The eight band levels from the most recent measurement. With them the home screen can draw the shape of your surroundings, and the sound can start already shaped on the next Play.
The morning line
How long the sound played last night, when it stopped, and the times at which Adapt raised or lowered the level. This is what produces "Played 7h 42m" on the home screen. It contains no audio.
Playback state for the widget
Whether the sound is playing, shared with the Home Screen widget through an app group that only this app and its widget can read.

Siri, Shortcuts and the widget

Start, Stop, and Toggle are App Intents. You can use them through Siri, the Shortcuts app, the Action button, or the Home Screen widget. In every case iOS runs the intent inside the Blanket app on your phone. The app receives no information about you from these requests, and sends none.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

A future version of the app may change what it does with the microphone or with stored information. If so, this page will be updated. The effective date at the top will change, and the version that the change applies to will be stated here.
